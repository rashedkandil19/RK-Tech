Frequently asked questions

Are domain and hosting included?

No. Domain registration and hosting are paid separately by the client, preferably through accounts they own.

How do payments work?

The standard arrangement is 50% to start and 50% before final handover, unless agreed otherwise in writing.

Can I request features outside a package?

Yes. Additional features are scoped and quoted separately before work begins.

How long does a website take?

Starter websites typically take 5–7 business days and Business websites 10–15 business days after receiving the required content. Complex projects need a custom timeline.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Each package includes the stated post-launch support period. Ongoing maintenance can be arranged separately.
