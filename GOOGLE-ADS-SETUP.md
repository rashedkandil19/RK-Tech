# Google Ads & Analytics setup

The site emits these `dataLayer` events:
- `lead_form_submit` — the visitor submitted the enquiry form and WhatsApp was opened.
- `whatsapp_click` — the visitor clicked a WhatsApp link or submitted the enquiry form to open WhatsApp.
- `phone_click` — the visitor clicked the phone link.

UTM parameters and GCLID are retained in session storage for the current browser session and included in the WhatsApp enquiry message when available.

## Before launch
1. Create a Google Tag Manager container and add its ID to `js/config.js` as `GTM_ID`.
2. In GTM, configure GA4 and Google Ads tags and map the events above to the conversions you actually want to measure.
3. Alternatively, for direct GA4 without GTM, set `GA4_ID` in `js/config.js`. Do not configure both paths unless intentional.
4. Add your Google Ads conversion ID and conversion label in GTM. These are account-specific and cannot be generated by the website source alone.
5. Test with Tag Assistant and GA4 DebugView. Exclude test activity from reporting.
6. Keep `lead_form_submit` and WhatsApp clicks as secondary/contact-intent actions until you have a backend or CRM that confirms actual enquiries and paid customers.
7. For offline conversion imports, store GCLID alongside the lead in a secure CRM and upload only after the sale is confirmed and the applicable consent/legal basis is in place.

## Contact form limitation
The static form opens WhatsApp with the submitted details. The visitor must press **Send** in WhatsApp. This does not confirm that a message was delivered, and the site does not currently store submissions in its own database or send automatic emails. Add a backend/CRM integration if you need reliable lead storage, delivery confirmation, and qualified-lead tracking.

## Final checks
- Replace the canonical base URL if you move to a custom domain.
- Add real GTM / GA4 / Google Ads IDs; placeholders are intentionally blank.
- Verify privacy, consent, and data-retention requirements applicable to your business and audience.
